Now → mid fall

Learning to build like a professional

Ibukun starts with real programming fundamentals and picks up the habits professional developers use every day — organizing code, tracking changes, testing her work. She'll finish this stretch with her first real project saved to her own portfolio.

Winter → spring

Building tools and websites

She starts automating everyday tasks with code, then builds the two halves of a real website: the part users see (frontend) and the part that stores and serves data (backend). By spring, she'll have a small working website of her own.

Summer

Into AI and machine learning

With more free time over the summer, she moves into the part her dad's been asking about: teaching computers to find patterns in data and make predictions. She'll train her first real machine learning model.

Fall → winter, year two

Cloud, and the final project

She learns to launch her projects on the same cloud platforms companies like Netflix and Amazon run on. The program ends with a capstone: one complete project — website, AI model, and cloud hosting — built entirely by her, live on the internet.
